A community that builds things, together.

DevSphere started as a small group of friends sharing laptops after class. Today it is a student-led, GDG-inspired community for developers, designers, and curious technology enthusiasts. You don't need experience to join. You need curiosity.

Questions, answered

Do I need coding experience to join?

No. Many members started with zero experience. Workshops are paced for beginners, and project squads include roles for designers and writers too.

Does it cost anything?

Membership and all events listed here are free in this concept.

Is DevSphere an official Google Developer Group?

No. DevSphere is a fictional, GDG-inspired community concept and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Can I propose my own project?

Yes. Pitch it at any Build Night. If at least three members want to work on it, we'll form a squad.

Your next project needs a team.

Join DevSphere, pick an event, or follow along by email. It takes under a minute.
